TERMS OF REFERENCE
THE BORDER MANAGEMENT AUTHORITY (BMA) HEREBY INVITES SUITABLY QUALIFIED SERVICE
PROVIDERS TO SUBMIT PROPOSALS FOR THE DESIGN, DEVELOPMENT, IMPLEMENTATION,
SUPPORT, AND MAINTENANCE OF THE ADMINISTRATIVE FINES MANAGEMENT SYSTEM.
1.BACKGROUND AND MANDATE
The Border Management Authority (BMA) is established in terms of the Border Management
Authority Act, 2020 as a single, integrated authority responsible for border law enforcement.
3.SCOPE OF WORK
The Service Provider shall provide a complete end-to-end solution for the design, development,
implementation, support, and maintenance of a digital Fines Management System.
3.1.1 Project Management
The Service Provider shall develop and maintain a detailed project plan with defined milestones.
3.1.3 Integration
Financial systems for payment reconciliation and financial reporting (e.g Sage X3),
Microsoft Entra ID for authentication and Single Sign-On (SSO),
Other systems as may be required through secure API-based integration mechanisms.
Go-Live support shall be provided for a period of two weeks.
3.1.5 Quality Assurance and Testing
Conduct unit, system, integration, performance, and participate in user acceptance testing.
